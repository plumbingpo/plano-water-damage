Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • A vapor barrier is worth considering in a crawl space, which also needs correct ventilation
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule

Our Mold Remediation Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle mold remediation from start to finish:

1

Mold Inspection and Sampling

A certified technician goes through the property to find mold both in plain view and out of sight, traces the moisture feeding it and, where useful, takes surface or air samples for a lab to analyze.

2

Sealing Off the Work Area

HEPA air scrubbers and polyethylene barriers put the work zone under negative air pressure, which keeps spores from reaching clean parts of the building while we remove the mold.

3

Removing Mold and Treating Surfaces

Affected materials are carefully removed and disposed of following EPA guidelines. The structure left in place gets treated with antimicrobials of professional strength.

4

Fixing the Moisture and Verifying Results

The moisture source behind the problem is corrected, any drying still needed is finished, and containment comes down only after post-remediation verification confirms the work was successful.

The Benefits of Professional Mold Remediation

This is not a job to take on yourself. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Signs of concealed moisture such as paint that bubbles, wallpaper that peels or materials that warp
  • Persistent musty or earthy odors, especially in enclosed or humid areas
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Mold or mildew that comes back soon after you clean it
  • Condensation forming regularly on windows, walls or cold surfaces
  • Long-running hidden leaks, for example at a dishwasher line, sink drain or roof flashing
