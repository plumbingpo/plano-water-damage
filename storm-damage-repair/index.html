Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage

Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
