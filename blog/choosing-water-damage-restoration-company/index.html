Selecting a Restoration Professional in Plano, TX

Home / Blog / How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Plano, TX

After water damage at a Plano, TX property, few choices matter more than which restoration company you hire. Your out-of-pocket cost, the pace of the work, the thoroughness of the repair, and the odds of secondary trouble such as mold months later all depend on who you hire. Not every outfit is qualified, and hiring the wrong one can mean inflated invoices, a job left incomplete, or something worse. Use the points below to tell a strong restoration company apart from a weak one.

Verify IICRC Certification

Restoration credentials are measured against the IICRC, short for the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification. Technicians who hold it have gone through demanding coursework on the science of water damage restoration: correct extraction, structural drying methods, measuring moisture, and preventing mold. Ask any restoration company you're considering whether their technicians hold current IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Make Sure They Are Insured and Licensed

Workers' compensation and general liability coverage are must-haves for a restoration contractor on your property. Request certificates of insurance and verify they're current. Without proper coverage, you could be liable for injuries or additional property damage that occur during the restoration. Also verify that the company holds any business licenses required by Plano or Texas regulations.

Evaluate Response Time and Availability

A water loss gets worse the longer it waits. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. Look for a company that truly answers emergencies 24/7 and can get to your area quickly. Ask specifically about their response time to Plano and confirm that their emergency line is answered by a live person, not a voicemail or answering service that adds delay.

Look at Their Equipment and Drying Process

Professional restoration requires professional equipment. Ask what type of extraction equipment the company uses (truck-mounted units are superior to portable-only operations), what type of dehumidifiers they deploy (LGR units are the industry standard), and whether they use thermal imaging for moisture detection. Also ask about their drying monitoring process: reputable companies monitor moisture levels daily and adjust equipment based on readings, not just place equipment and hope for the best.

Look Into Reviews and Past Customers

You can learn a lot about past performance from what customers post online. Look at the BBB, Yelp, and Google, and watch for steady patterns in quality, professionalism, and how satisfied customers were. The most useful reviews talk about communication, punctuality, help with insurance, and how the finished restoration turned out. It is also fair to ask for the names of recent Plano water damage customers you can contact.

Understand Pricing and Insurance Billing

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Xactimate and similar industry-standard pricing programs, recognized and accepted by insurance carriers, are used by many quality contractors. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. A demand for big upfront money, or a refusal to deal with your insurer, is a warning sign.
