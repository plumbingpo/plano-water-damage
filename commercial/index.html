Risk Assessment

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Sudden failures caused by old plumbing, poor waterproofing, or moisture nobody can see may lead to property damage, injuries, and expensive lawsuits. A Plano Restoration Co risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. You receive a written report that lists each risk, ranks it by priority, and spells out the preventive steps to take.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Insurance companies and courts tend to favor a property owner who acts on expert recommendations and has inspections done regularly by professionals. We recommend annual moisture assessments for all commercial properties, with more frequent inspections for high-risk areas such as basements, mechanical rooms, and aging plumbing systems.
