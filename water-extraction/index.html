Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Plano Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout Plano using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Save (737) 283-9062 in your contacts now, and you can reach us at once when something floods
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor

Signs You Need Water Extraction in Plano

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.
